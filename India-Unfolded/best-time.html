BEST TIME TO VISIT INDIA

Discover when to explore India's beaches, mountains, cities, wildlife and cultural destinations.

WHEN SHOULD YOU VISIT INDIA?

India is a year-round destination, but the ideal time to visit depends on where you are going and what you want to experience. The country's climate varies greatly between regions.

Before travelling, check the weather for your specific destination and plan your activities around local conditions.

INDIA THROUGH THE SEASONS

❄️

Winter

October – February

Winter is one of the most popular travel periods for many parts of India. Northern plains are generally more comfortable for sightseeing, while Himalayan destinations can experience cold weather and snow.

Best for

  • Rajasthan
  • Delhi & Agra
  • Varanasi
  • Goa
  • Kerala
  • Himalayan snow destinations

🌸

Spring

February – April

Spring brings pleasant conditions to many regions. It is a good period for sightseeing, festivals and exploring landscapes before the hotter months arrive.

Best for

  • Himalayan regions
  • Kerala
  • North India
  • Wildlife experiences
  • Festivals and culture
Explore seasonal information →
☀️

Summer

April – June

Summer can be hot across much of the plains, but mountain and hill destinations can provide a cooler escape. Some wildlife destinations are also popular during this period.

Best for

  • Shimla
  • Manali
  • Darjeeling
  • Ooty
  • Munnar
  • Wildlife parks
Check IMD forecasts →
🌧️

Monsoon

June – September

Monsoon transforms many parts of India with greenery, waterfalls and dramatic landscapes. However, heavy rainfall can affect road travel and outdoor activities in some regions.

Best for

  • Kerala
  • Western Ghats
  • Goa
  • Meghalaya
  • Monsoon photography
  • Waterfalls

BEST TIME BY TRAVEL STYLE

Mountain Trips

Spring and summer are popular for many Himalayan destinations, while winter is ideal for snow experiences.

Beach Holidays

Winter is a popular period for Goa and many other beach destinations.

Wildlife

Many national parks are especially popular during the drier and warmer months, subject to park rules.

Heritage & Culture

Cooler months are generally more comfortable for exploring forts, monuments, temples and cities.

CHECK THE WEATHER BEFORE YOU GO

Weather can change quickly, especially in mountain, coastal and monsoon regions. Always check the latest forecast before travelling.

PLAN YOUR BUDGET

Understand your travel costs and plan an India trip that fits your style and budget.

HOW MUCH DOES AN INDIA TRIP COST?

Your travel budget depends on your destination, accommodation, transport, food and activities. Use these ranges as a simple starting point for planning.

CHOOSE YOUR TRAVEL STYLE

🎒

Budget Travel

₹1,000 – ₹2,000 / day

Travel simply while keeping your daily expenses low.

Usually includes

  • Hostels or budget stays
  • Local food
  • Public transport
  • Low-cost activities
🧳

Mid-Range Travel

₹2,500 – ₹5,000 / day

A comfortable balance between price and convenience.

Usually includes

  • Comfortable hotels
  • Restaurants & cafés
  • Trains, buses or occasional flights
  • Popular attractions
✨

Comfort Travel

₹6,000+ / day

More comfort, flexibility and premium experiences.

Usually includes

  • Premium hotels or resorts
  • Restaurants and fine dining
  • Flights and private transfers
  • Premium experiences

WHERE YOUR MONEY GOES

These are broad planning categories. Actual prices vary by destination, season and travel style.

🏨

Accommodation

Hostels, guesthouses, hotels and resorts are available across different price ranges.

Save money:

Compare locations and book early during busy seasons.

🍛

Food

India offers everything from inexpensive local meals to cafés and fine dining.

Save money:

Try local restaurants and regional food.

🚆

Transport

Trains, buses, metros, flights and taxis provide different options for getting around.

Save money:

Use public transport where it is convenient.

🎟️

Activities

Museums, monuments, tours, adventure activities and experiences can add to your daily spending.

Save money:

Choose a few priority experiences instead of everything.

SIMPLE WAYS TO SAVE MONEY

01

Travel Off-Peak

Consider travelling outside the busiest periods when prices and crowds may be lower.

02

Use Public Transport

Trains, buses and metro systems can reduce local transportation costs.

03

Eat Local

Regional restaurants and local food can be affordable and memorable.

04

Book Early

Planning transport and accommodation ahead can give you more choices.

TRAVEL TIPS & ESSENTIALS

Simple information to help you travel across India with confidence, comfort and better preparation.

TRAVEL SMARTER

A little preparation can make your journey easier. From packing and payments to safety and weather, these tips will help you prepare for your Indian adventure.

🎒

Before You Travel

Plan your basic route, accommodation and transportation before starting your journey.

  • Keep your travel documents ready.
  • Confirm hotel and transport bookings.
  • Check the weather at your destination.
  • Save important addresses and contact numbers.
Explore India Travel Information →
🧳

What to Pack

Pack according to the destination, season and activities planned during your trip.

  • Comfortable clothes and walking shoes.
  • Basic medicines and personal essentials.
  • Phone charger and power bank.
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses and a water bottle.
  • A small day bag for sightseeing.
Plan Your Visit →
💳

Money & Payments

India supports several payment methods, but carrying some cash can still be useful, especially outside major cities.

  • Keep some Indian Rupees for small purchases.
  • UPI and cards are widely used in many places.
  • Inform your bank before international travel.
  • Check exchange rates before converting money.
Reserve Bank of India →
🛡️

Stay Safe

Staying aware of your surroundings and keeping important belongings secure can make your journey safer.

  • Keep valuables secure in crowded places.
  • Use trusted transportation services.
  • Share your itinerary with someone you trust.
  • Keep copies of important documents.
  • Be careful with unfamiliar food and water.
Emergency Number 112 →
🌦️

Weather & Seasons

India's climate varies greatly between regions, so check local conditions before travelling.

  • Summer can be very hot in many regions.
  • Monsoon can bring heavy rainfall.
  • Winter is cooler, especially in northern India.
  • Mountain weather can change quickly.
India Meteorological Department →
🚆

Transport Tips

Choose transportation according to your destination, budget and available travel time.

  • Book popular trains in advance.
  • Check your station and boarding point carefully.
  • Arrive early for flights and long-distance buses.
  • Use official or trusted transport services.
Explore Getting Around →

Stay Connected

Having reliable connectivity makes navigation, communication and online bookings much easier.

  • Keep your phone charged during sightseeing.
  • Download important maps before travelling.
  • Keep hotel and transport details offline.
  • Use trusted Wi-Fi networks.

Responsible Travel

Respecting local communities, culture and nature helps keep destinations enjoyable for everyone.

  • Respect local customs and traditions.
  • Avoid littering and reduce plastic waste.
  • Follow rules at heritage and religious sites.
  • Support local businesses when possible.

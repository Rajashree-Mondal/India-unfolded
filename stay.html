WHERE TO STAY IN INDIA

From luxury hotels and peaceful resorts to homestays and budget-friendly accommodation, find a stay that suits your journey.

FIND YOUR PERFECT STAY

India offers accommodation for every kind of traveller. Choose from luxurious city hotels, relaxing resorts, traditional homestays and affordable stays.

Luxury Hotels

Experience elegant rooms, excellent service and premium facilities in some of India's finest hotels.

Best for

Couples, special occasions and travellers looking for comfort and premium experiences.

Resorts

Resorts offer a relaxing escape surrounded by beaches, mountains, forests or countryside.

Best for

Relaxing holidays, family trips and weekend getaways.

Homestays

Stay with local hosts and experience the culture, food and everyday life of the region.

Best for

Cultural experiences, slow travel and travellers who prefer a personal atmosphere.

Budget Stays

Affordable hotels, hostels and guesthouses make travelling across India easier on a budget.

Best for

Students, backpackers and budget-conscious travellers.

Heritage Stays

Stay in historic palaces, havelis and restored buildings that showcase India's rich heritage.

Best for

History lovers, cultural travellers and memorable experiences.

Hostels

Hostels provide affordable accommodation and opportunities to meet fellow travellers.

Best for

Solo travellers, backpackers and young travellers.

SMART STAYING TIPS

📍 Choose the Location

Stay close to major attractions or transport connections to save time during your trip.

⭐ Check Reviews

Read recent guest reviews and check ratings before booking your accommodation.

💰 Compare Prices

Compare different booking options and check what facilities are included in the price.

🔐 Check Facilities

Check important facilities such as Wi-Fi, breakfast, parking and air conditioning.

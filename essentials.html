TRAVEL ESSENTIALS

Everything you need to pack and prepare for a comfortable journey across India.

PACK SMART. TRAVEL BETTER.

A little preparation can make your journey smoother. From important documents to everyday essentials, use this guide to prepare before exploring India.

WHAT TO PACK

📄

Documents

Keep important travel documents safe and easily accessible throughout your journey.

  • Government ID
  • Tickets & reservations
  • Hotel details
  • Emergency contacts
👕

Clothing

Choose clothing according to the destination, season and activities planned.

  • Comfortable everyday clothes
  • Light layers
  • Comfortable footwear
  • Rain protection when needed
🧴

Personal Care

Carry everyday personal-care items so you can stay comfortable while travelling.

  • Toiletries
  • Sunscreen
  • Hand sanitizer
  • Personal medicines
🔌

Electronics

Keep your devices charged and ready for navigation, communication and photography.

  • Phone & charger
  • Power bank
  • Earphones
  • Travel adapter if required
💳

Money & Payments

Carry a convenient combination of digital payment methods and some backup cash.

  • Debit or credit card
  • UPI where available
  • Some emergency cash
  • Secure wallet
🎒

Day Bag

A small day bag keeps your frequently used items easy to reach while sightseeing.

  • Water bottle
  • Phone
  • Wallet
  • Small essentials

PACK ACCORDING TO YOUR DESTINATION

India has very different climates and landscapes, so your packing list should match where you are going.

Mountains

Warm layers, comfortable shoes, a light jacket and weather-appropriate accessories.

Beaches

Light clothing, sun protection, comfortable sandals and a reusable water bottle.

Heritage Cities

Comfortable walking shoes, breathable clothing and sun protection for sightseeing.

Monsoon Travel

Rain protection, quick-drying clothing and footwear suitable for wet conditions.

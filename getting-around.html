CHOOSE YOUR WAY TO TRAVEL

India offers many ways to explore its cities, mountains, beaches and heritage destinations.

🚆

Train Travel

One of the best ways to travel long distances in India. Trains connect major cities and offer scenic journeys.

Best for

Long-distance journeys and budget-friendly travel.

✈️

Flights

Domestic flights are ideal when you want to cover long distances quickly.

Best for

Saving time on long-distance journeys.

🚌

Bus Travel

Buses connect cities, towns and destinations that may not have convenient train connections.

Best for

Short and medium-distance journeys.

🚇

Metro

Metro systems make travelling around major Indian cities faster and easier.

Best for

Exploring large cities and avoiding road traffic.

🚕

Taxi & Cab

Taxis and app-based cabs are convenient for sightseeing and local travel.

Best for

Door-to-door travel and local sightseeing.

🚗

Self Drive

A road trip gives you the freedom to explore destinations at your own pace.

Best for

Road trips and flexible travel plans.
